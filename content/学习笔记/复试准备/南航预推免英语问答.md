# 南航预推免面试：常见英语问答

> 适用背景：雷显秋，来自江西南昌，中国矿业大学计算机科学与技术专业，赴南京航空航天大学参加预推免面试。
> 学校英文名：**Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics (NUAA)**。
> 本文根据当前目录中的自我介绍和项目材料整理，不是南航官方题库。成绩、排名和项目状态请以面试时的最新情况为准。

## 使用建议

- 优先练习第 1—8、11—14、20 题。普通题控制在 20—40 秒，自我介绍和项目介绍可以稍长。
- 先记住中文思路，再用自己的话说英文，不必逐字背诵。老师追问时再展开细节。
- 家乡介绍已按江西南昌准备。第 9、10、18、19、21、22 题涉及个人偏好或意愿，是建议表达，使用前按真实情况调整。
- 开场按时间使用 “Good morning” 或 “Good afternoon”。不写死本科年级，避免沿用旧材料中的 “third-year”。

## 一、个人背景与家乡

### 1. Could you briefly introduce yourself?（请简要介绍自己）

**参考回答：**

Good morning, professors. My name is Lei Xianqiu, and I come from Nanchang, Jiangxi Province. I am an undergraduate student majoring in Computer Science and Technology at China University of Mining and Technology. My GPA is 4.42 out of 5, and I rank 21st out of 191 students in my major. I lead an undergraduate research project on medical image analysis, where I work on model training and evaluation. I also received an Honorable Mention in the Mathematical Contest in Modeling. I am interested in deep learning and computer vision, and I hope to continue my studies at NUAA. Thank you.

**中文思路：** 姓名、江西南昌 → 本科学校和专业 → 成绩 → 项目和竞赛 → 研究兴趣与南航。

### 2. Where are you from? Could you introduce your hometown?（你来自哪里？介绍一下家乡）

**参考回答：**

I come from Nanchang, the capital of Jiangxi Province. It is a city with a long history, located on the Gan River. One of its most famous landmarks is Tengwang Pavilion, which is closely connected with classical Chinese literature. Nanchang is also known for its local food, such as rice noodles and soup cooked in clay pots. For me, it is a special place because it is my hometown.

**中文思路：** 江西省会南昌 → 赣江、历史 → 滕王阁 → 南昌米粉和瓦罐汤 → 家乡感情。

**简短版：** I come from Nanchang, the capital of Jiangxi Province. It is famous for Tengwang Pavilion and its local food, especially rice noodles and soup cooked in clay pots.

### 3. What would you recommend to someone visiting Nanchang?（去南昌旅游，你会推荐什么？）

**参考回答：**

I would recommend visiting Tengwang Pavilion. It is one of the best-known landmarks in Nanchang and a good place to learn about traditional Chinese culture. Visitors can also take a walk along the Gan River and enjoy the city views. For breakfast, I would suggest trying Nanchang rice noodles with soup cooked in clay pots. Some local dishes are quite spicy, so visitors can ask for less chili.

**中文思路：** 滕王阁和传统文化 → 赣江沿岸 → 南昌拌粉、瓦罐汤 → 辣度提醒。

**使用提醒：** 这是旅行推荐，没有声称你亲自去过。被问旅行经历时只讲真实经历。

### 4. Could you introduce your undergraduate university?（介绍一下你的本科学校）

**参考回答：**

I study at China University of Mining and Technology in Xuzhou, Jiangsu Province. My major is Computer Science and Technology. During my undergraduate studies, I have taken courses in mathematics, programming, and computer science. Research projects and competitions have also given me opportunities to put what I learned into practice and work with other students.

**中文思路：** 中国矿业大学、徐州 → 专业 → 课程基础 → 项目实践。

## 二、选择南航与读研动机

### 5. Why do you want to study at NUAA?（为什么选择南航？）

**参考回答：**

I am interested in NUAA because of its strong engineering background and its focus on practical problems. My undergraduate project showed me how computer vision can be applied to medical images. I would like to build on that experience and learn how to conduct more rigorous research. I hope to join a research group at NUAA that matches my interests in deep learning and computer vision.

**中文思路：** 工科背景和实际问题 → 本科项目引出研究兴趣 → 希望接受更系统的科研训练。

**使用提醒：** 这是通用动机稿。确定报考学院和导师后，可补一句已核实的研究匹配点，不要泛称某位老师从事医学影像研究。

### 6. What do you know about NUAA?（你对南航有什么了解？）

**参考回答：**

NUAA stands for Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics. It is well known for its strengths in aeronautics, astronautics, and engineering. What interests me is the connection between computing and engineering applications. I would like to explore how methods in artificial intelligence can help solve practical problems through further study and research.

**中文思路：** 能说出校名全称 → 航空航天和工科特色 → 计算机与工程应用的结合。

### 7. Why do you want to pursue a master's degree?（为什么想读研？）

**参考回答：**

My undergraduate project made me realize that running a model is only the first step in research. I also need to understand why it works, when it fails, and how to evaluate it properly. I want to pursue a master's degree to strengthen my theoretical foundation and develop the ability to investigate a research problem independently.

**中文思路：** 本科项目让自己意识到不足 → 从会运行模型到理解和评价模型 → 培养独立研究能力。

### 8. Why would you like to study in Nanjing?（为什么想来南京读书？）

**参考回答：**

Nanjing has a rich history and many universities, which makes it an attractive place to study. I already study in Jiangsu, so continuing my education in Nanjing feels like a natural next step. However, my main consideration is the university and the research opportunities. I hope to find a suitable research group and grow as a researcher at NUAA.

**中文思路：** 历史文化和高校氛围 → 已在江苏读书 → 最看重学校与研究匹配。

## 三、学习与个人特点

### 9. Why did you choose computer science?（为什么选择计算机专业？）

**参考回答，需确认符合真实动机：**

I chose computer science because I enjoy logical thinking and solving problems. Programming allows me to turn an idea into something that actually works. As I learned more, I became interested in artificial intelligence, especially computer vision. My experience with medical image analysis further showed me how computing can contribute to problems beyond computer science itself.

**中文思路：** 逻辑和解题兴趣 → 编程实现想法 → 逐渐关注 AI 和计算机视觉。

### 10. What is your favorite course?（你最喜欢哪门课？）

**参考回答，若确实喜欢数学可使用：**

One of my favorite courses is Advanced Mathematics, in which I received a score of 100. I enjoyed learning how to analyze problems step by step. Concepts such as derivatives and gradients also helped me understand optimization in machine learning. This course gave me a useful foundation for studying more advanced topics.

**中文思路：** 高等数学 → 满分作为支撑 → 导数、梯度和机器学习的联系。

**可能追问：** “How is mathematics used in deep learning?”

**简短回答：** Derivatives are used to compute gradients, which tell us how the loss changes with the model parameters. An optimizer then uses these gradients to update the parameters.

### 11. What are your strengths?（你的优点是什么？）

**参考回答：**

One of my strengths is that I am patient and do not give up easily. When I face a difficult problem in my studies, I try to solve it step by step. If one method does not work, I try another or ask others for advice. I believe patience helps me keep learning and making progress.

**中文思路：** 有耐心、不轻易放弃 → 遇到难题逐步解决 → 换方法或请教别人 → 持续学习和进步。

### 12. What is one of your weaknesses?（你的不足是什么？）

**参考回答：**

One of my weaknesses is that I sometimes spend too much time on small details. For example, when making slides, I may spend too long adjusting the layout. This can slow down my work. To improve, I am learning to set time limits and finish the main tasks first.

**中文思路：** 有时在细节上花太多时间 → 例如反复调整幻灯片排版 → 影响效率 → 设定时间限制，先完成主要任务。

## 四、项目与科研经历

### 13. Could you introduce your research project?（介绍一下你的科研项目）

**参考回答：**

My main project focuses on medical image analysis for retinal diseases. We worked on classifying retinal images and segmenting blood vessels and small lesions. I was the team leader and was responsible for model training, evaluation, and experimental analysis using PyTorch. We adapted existing deep learning models to these tasks. The project taught me that reliable data processing and evaluation are just as important as the model itself.

**中文思路：** 眼底图像分析 → 分类和分割任务 → 第一负责人、训练评估 → 对数据和评价可靠性的认识。

**使用提醒：** 分类、血管分割、微动脉瘤分割使用不同的数据和模型配置。被问细节时分别说明，不要混成同一套实验。

### 14. What was your specific contribution to the project?（你具体做了什么？）

**参考回答：**

I led a team of five students. My main responsibility was the experimental work, including model training, evaluation, and analysis for the three tasks. I used PyTorch to adapt and run existing models. One teammate worked on the front end, and the other three focused on the project report. My contribution was mainly in implementation and experimentation, rather than proposing a completely new model.

**中文思路：** 五人团队 → 自己负责三条任务线的实验 → 其他成员分工 → 如实界定贡献。

### 15. What was the biggest challenge in your project?（项目中最大的困难是什么？）

**参考回答：**

One major challenge was segmenting very small lesions in retinal images. These lesions occupy only a small number of pixels, so important details can be lost when an image is resized. We used image patches and a multi-scale model to address this problem. However, the evaluation still needed improvement, so I would not draw a firm conclusion about the performance of that part.

**中文思路：** 病灶小、缩放丢细节 → 图像分块和多尺度模型 → 承认该分支评价仍有问题。

**可能追问：** “What would you improve?”

**简短回答：** I would first check that the images and masks are correctly matched and processed. Then I would use a verified evaluation pipeline and include lesion-level evaluation.

### 16. What did you learn from mathematical modeling?（数学建模带给你什么收获？）

**参考回答：**

Our team received an Honorable Mention in the Mathematical Contest in Modeling. We studied an elimination system involving judges' scores and audience votes. Since the actual audience votes were not available, we treated them as hidden variables and used Bayesian methods to estimate them. I worked on modeling, implementation, experiments, and writing. I learned to state assumptions clearly and explain the uncertainty in a model's results.

**中文思路：** 美赛 H 奖 → 不可观测投票 → 隐变量和贝叶斯估计 → 分工与收获。

### 17. What are your research interests?（你的研究兴趣是什么？）

**参考回答：**

I am interested in deep learning and computer vision, especially medical image analysis. My project experience also made me interested in the reliability of AI systems. For example, I would like to understand how a model performs on data from different sources and how we can identify uncertain predictions. I am open to refining my research direction with my supervisor.

**中文思路：** 深度学习、视觉、医学图像 → 跨来源数据的泛化与不确定性 → 接受导师指导。

## 五、日常生活与沟通

### 18. What do you do in your spare time?（你有什么业余爱好？）

**参考回答：**

In my spare time, I enjoy walking and reading. Walking helps me relax after studying and gives me a break from sitting in front of a computer. Reading helps me learn new things and see things from different points of view. Both activities help me feel relaxed and refreshed.

**可能追问：** “Could you introduce a book you have read?”（能介绍一本你读过的书吗？）或 “What is your favorite book?”（你最喜欢的书是什么？）

One of my favorite books is The Old Man and the Sea by Ernest Hemingway. It tells the story of an old fisherman who struggles to catch a huge fish. I admire his courage because he does not give up when facing difficulties. The story reminds me to be patient and keep trying in my studies.

**中文思路：** 海明威的《老人与海》 → 老渔夫努力捕获大鱼 → 欣赏他的勇气和不放弃的精神 → 提醒自己在学习中保持耐心、坚持尝试。

### 19. How do you deal with pressure?（你如何应对压力？）

**参考回答，按实际习惯调整：**

When I feel under pressure, I first break the task into smaller steps and decide what needs to be done first. This helps me focus on something manageable. If I get stuck, I review what I have tried and discuss the problem with others when necessary. I also take short breaks so that I can return with a clearer mind.

**中文思路：** 拆任务、排优先级 → 复盘和交流 → 适当休息。

## 七、听不清或答不上来时

### 24. 没听清问题

I'm sorry, could you please repeat the question?

不好意思，能请您重复一下问题吗？

### 25. 听到了，但不理解问题含义

Could you please rephrase the question?

您可以换一种说法吗？

也可以先确认：

Do you mean my contribution to the project?

您是指我在项目中的具体贡献吗？

### 26. 需要短暂思考

Let me think for a moment.

请让我想一下。

### 27. 确实不知道，或只知道一部分

I'm not familiar enough with this topic to give a precise answer. I would need to study it further.

我对这个问题还不够熟悉，暂时不能给出准确回答，需要进一步学习。

如果确实有部分思路，可以接：

My current understanding is that ...

我目前的理解是……

**使用提醒：** 不确定就明确说明，再讲自己能确定的部分，不编造结论或实验数字。
