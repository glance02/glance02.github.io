## 中文版

### 1. 背景

糖尿病视网膜病变是糖尿病患者最常见的并发症之一，也是劳动年龄人口致盲的主要原因之一。它的特点是早期几乎没有症状，等到患者自觉视力下降时，往往已经错过了最佳干预窗口。因此，定期筛查是降低致盲率的关键。

但现实问题是：眼底彩照的判读依赖眼科医生，而基层医疗机构的眼科资源普遍不足，大量患者无法及时得到筛查。眼底彩照本身采集成本低、无创、易标准化，如果能用深度学习自动完成病变分级和病灶识别，就能在很大程度上缓解医生资源不足的问题，把筛查前移到基层。

这也是我们做这个项目的出发点：不是单纯刷模型指标，而是希望搭出一套能跑通、能展示、有临床参考价值的眼底图像分析流程。

### 2. 数据集和划分

这个项目包含三条相对独立的数据链，用的不是同一个数据集。这一点在面试里必须先说清楚，否则老师会觉得你对项目结构没有概念。

- **DR 二分类**：基于公开的 Diabetic Retinopathy Arranged 数据集。我把它下载下来后自己做了整理和预处理，形成目录 `dataset260106`。标签是二分类（0 = 健康，1 = DR 阳性）：训练集 4529 张，其中正类 2264、负类 2265；测试集 1135 张，其中正类 568、负类 567。两个集合的类别比例都接近 50:50，属于均衡分布。
- **血管分割**：公开数据集 DRIVE。
- **微动脉瘤检测与分割**：公开数据集 IDRiD 的微动脉瘤子集，测试集为 27 张全幅眼底彩照。

数据划分上，血管分割和微动脉瘤沿用各自数据集的官方划分；DR 二分类按约 8:2 划分出训练集和测试集。

下载后的整理工作主要是把图像按二分类标签组织成目录、划分训练集和测试集，并生成训练脚本直接读取的标签文件（格式为 `路径;标签`）。

预处理方面，原始图像尺寸为 1024×768，训练和推理时统一缩放到模型输入尺寸（二分类 224×224，分割 512×512），未保持长宽比，之后按 Fundus 模态的均值和方差做标准化。微动脉瘤分支因为病灶极小，采用 96×96 的 patch 方式训练，训练集共抽取 19 万个 patch；测试时用步长 20 的重叠滑窗推理，再对重叠区域取平均重建整幅预测图。

### 3. 方法

整体流程是：眼底彩照 → 数据预处理 → DR 分级识别 → 血管分割与微动脉瘤检测 → 结果可视化与辅助解读。

DR 二分类部分，我用了 VisionFM 多模态多任务眼科基础模型。它的思路是在大规模眼科图像上预训练，学习通用的眼底表征，再迁移到具体的下游任务。相比从零训练，这种迁移方式在标注数据有限时更有效。我在此基础上构建了二分类流程：取 ViT 编码器最后 4 层的 CLS token 拼接成特征向量，接一个 3 层 MLP 分类头，输入尺寸 224×224，加载 VisionFM 的眼底预训练权重做微调。

微动脉瘤部分，我参照了一篇微动脉瘤分割论文的公开源码（IDRiD 分割分支），复现并跑通了它的训练与测试流程。主干是 MSRNet（多尺度残差网络），在残差块上加了 SE 注意力模块，训练目标是在 patch 级别做二分类，损失函数用 CrossEntropyLoss，优化器 Adam，学习率前 150 轮 1e-3、之后降到 1e-4。选择 patch 方式而不是整图直接分割，是因为微动脉瘤在整幅眼底图上往往只有几个像素，整图下采样后这些目标会在池化过程中直接消失；改成 96×96 的小图块后，病灶在 patch 内的像素占比大幅提高，模型才有机会学到它。

血管分割部分，我同样以 VisionFM 的 ViT 编码器为主干，取第 3、5、7、11 层的 patch token 作为多尺度特征，接 UNETR 解码头，输入 512×512，最后上采样回原图尺寸输出血管掩码。

实现全部用 PyTorch 完成。评估指标方面，分类任务用 Accuracy、F1、AUC 和 AP，分割任务用 Dice 和 IoU（Jaccard）。

### 4. 我写了哪部分代码

作为项目第一负责人，我负责的是 DR 分类与病灶分割这两块算法，具体包括：

- 数据预处理与加载流程：写数据读取、清洗、增强和按患者划分数据集的代码，构建 PyTorch 的 Dataset 和 DataLoader
- DR 分级模型：复现并适配 VisionFM 的分类流程，完成训练、验证和测试脚本
- 微动脉瘤检测与分割：复现多尺度分割网络，完成细小病灶的检测与分割实验
- 实验评估：写指标计算代码，做错误样本分析，对比不同设置下的结果
- 结果可视化：把原图、预测热力图、分割 mask 叠加展示，便于判断模型是否真的看对了位置
- 平台流程：参与原型平台的模型调用流程设计，并引入千问模型对分类与分割结果生成文字解读

关于分工，团队一共 5 人，我是第一负责人。**三条任务线的模型训练、评估和实验分析全部由我独立完成**，包括数据预处理、二分类与血管分割的复现微调、微动脉瘤分支的复现与调参。另外 1 位同学负责 Web 原型的前端页面，其余 3 位同学分工完成项目报告的部分章节。

这样分工的结果是：项目里所有涉及模型和实验的部分我都能自己讲清楚，而这也正是老师最可能追问的地方。

### 5. 结果

三条任务线在内部独立测试集上的结果如下。

**DR 二分类**：AP 93.35%、Accuracy 80.70%、AUC 85.73%、F1 83.58%。

关于 AP 的口径需要说明：测试集是 50:50 均衡的，PR 曲线的随机基线就是 0.50，所以 AP 93.35% 相对基线有明显提升。不过训练脚本里用的是一次 macro 平均——`average_precision_score(target_one_hot, output, average='macro')`，也就是对两个类别分别求 AP 再取平均，而不是标准的二分类 average precision。在均衡数据上两者数值接近，但被问到时应当说明这一点。

**血管分割**：在 DRIVE 上 Dice 稳定达到 78.17%。

**微动脉瘤检测与分割**：这一支我们完成了方法复现和流程跑通，但**评测口径存在问题，指标不适合下结论，面试时不主动报数字，只讲方法**。原因见下。

问题出在两处。第一，论文源码里 `dice_average_sets` 接收的是整幅图展平后的一维像素数组，于是"逐样本求 Dice 再平均"变成了"逐个像素求 Dice 再平均"；带平滑项的逐像素 Dice 恒等于 `1 - 0.5×(1-准确率)`，只由准确率决定——模型全输出背景时这个值反而更高，所以它根本不是分割指标。

第二，用 Sensitivity 0.6083、Specificity 0.6850、Accuracy 0.6371 反推混淆矩阵，可以解出评测集中"正类像素"占到 **62.45%**；再用这个矩阵算出的 Precision、Dice、Jaccard 与报告值完全一致，说明这些数字确实来自同一个混淆矩阵。但眼底图里微动脉瘤的像素占比通常在 1% 以下，62% 不可能成立——**评测时用的 ground truth 很可能不是病灶掩码本身，而是加载错了掩码文件**。具体原因已经无法追溯（当时在服务器上训练，日志已删除）。

所以这一支的正确讲法是：**方法流程跑通了，但评估还不严谨，这是我们最需要重做的一部分。** 这样说既真实，也体现得出你能识别自己实验里的问题。

血管分割那一支：Dice 78.17% 意味着整体覆盖尚可，但细血管末梢容易断裂或漏分割，因为极细的血管在多次下采样中会损失细节。

需要说明的是，我们当时做的是逐例的可视化检查（把预测掩码叠加回原图对照），**没有做系统的分层统计**，比如按病变等级、图像质量或病灶大小分组统计错误率。这是回头看时觉得最应该补上的部分。

如果被追问进度，可以说：三条链路都完成了流程搭建、训练和初步验证，其中 DR 二分类和血管分割的指标相对稳定，微动脉瘤这一支还比较粗糙，是后续最需要改进的部分。

### 6. 不足

第一，数据规模和来源有限，而且每条任务线都只在自己那一个数据集上验证：DR 二分类只在整理后的 Diabetic Retinopathy Arranged 上、血管分割只在 DRIVE 上、微动脉瘤只在 IDRiD 上，都没有做跨数据集测试。医学图像模型最容易出现的问题就是在一个数据集上表现很好，换一套采集设备或换一个人群后性能明显下降，也就是域偏移问题。这是我们后续最需要补的实验。

第二，微动脉瘤这类微小病灶的分割精度受图像分辨率和标注质量影响很大。不同标注者对同一个病灶的边界判断可能不一致，这种标注噪声会直接反映到模型性能上。

第三，可解释性不足。我们引入了大语言模型对结果做文字解读，但这本质上是对输出结果的二次描述，**并不等于模型本身可解释**。如果模型判断错了，语言模型很可能会给出一个听起来合理但错误的解释，这里面存在幻觉风险。真正要做的是让模型的注意力或梯度能够定位到真实病灶位置，这是我们还没有做透的部分。

第四，系统只停留在原型阶段，没有经过真实临床环境的评估。原型平台验证的是流程能跑通，而不是临床有效性。要真正说明价值，需要和医院合作，在真实筛查场景下做前瞻性验证。

第五，评价体系本身也需要修正，这是三条链路里问题最大的一处。微动脉瘤那一支我们沿用了论文源码的评估脚本，复盘时发现两个问题：一是它的 Dice 在整图推理下变成了逐像素平均，数值只由准确率决定，根本不是分割指标；二是反推混淆矩阵后，评测集里"正样本"占到 62%，与微动脉瘤的稀疏性矛盾，说明 ground truth 很可能加载错了文件。因此这一支的指标我们不作为结论。这条链路的教训是：**先用对评价指标，再谈模型效果**——指标错了，模型再好也是自说自话。

第六，测试集的划分方式不够严格。DR 二分类的训练集和测试集是按图片随机划分的，没有按患者划分。数据集文件名里带有患者编号（例如 `10047_left` 和 `10047_right` 是同一患者的左右眼），实际统计发现共有 286 名患者的左右眼分别落进了训练集和测试集，占测试集患者总数的约 26%。同一患者的双眼在病变程度和成像条件上高度相关，这会让测试指标偏乐观。正确的做法是按患者分组划分，保证同一患者只出现在其中一个集合。这一点是我们复盘时才意识到的。

最后补充一句态度：这个项目的时间和算力都比较有限，所以三条链路都只做到跑通加初步验证，很多实验没能展开。如果继续做，我会优先补按患者分组划分和跨数据集验证——这两项改动本身不复杂，但能让结论可靠得多。

## English Version

### 1. Background

Diabetic retinopathy is one of the most common complications of diabetes and a leading cause of blindness among working-age adults. It is usually asymptomatic in its early stages, so by the time patients notice vision loss, the optimal window for intervention has often passed. Regular screening is therefore critical.

The practical problem is that reading fundus photographs depends on ophthalmologists, and primary healthcare facilities generally lack eye-care resources, leaving many patients unscreened. Fundus photography itself is low-cost, non-invasive and easy to standardize. If deep learning can automatically grade the lesions, screening can be pushed down to the primary-care level.

That was the motivation for our project: not simply to push a benchmark number, but to build a fundus image analysis pipeline that actually runs, can be demonstrated, and has clinical reference value.

### 2. Dataset and Splitting

The project actually involves three relatively independent data pipelines, and they do not share the same dataset. I should make this clear upfront, because otherwise it sounds like I have no clear picture of the project structure.

- **DR binary classification**: based on the public Diabetic Retinopathy Arranged dataset. I downloaded it and did my own organizing and preprocessing to produce the directory `dataset260106`. The labels are binary (0 = healthy, 1 = DR positive): the training set has 4,529 images (2,264 positive, 2,265 negative) and the test set has 1,135 images (568 positive, 567 negative). Both sets are close to a 50:50 balance.
- **Vessel segmentation**: the public DRIVE dataset.
- **Microaneurysm detection and segmentation**: the microaneurysm subset of the public IDRiD dataset, with 27 full fundus images in the test set.

Splitting follows the official splits of DRIVE and IDRiD respectively. The DR binary task was split roughly 8:2 into training and test sets.

After downloading, my organizing work consisted mainly of arranging the images into directories by their binary labels, splitting them into training and test sets, and generating the label files that the training pipeline reads directly (in the format `path;label`).

For preprocessing, the original images are 1024×768; at training and inference they are resized to each model's input size (224×224 for classification, 512×512 for segmentation) without preserving the aspect ratio, then normalized using the Fundus modality statistics. Because microaneurysms are extremely small, that branch is trained on 96×96 patches, with 190,000 patches sampled from the training set. At test time we use overlapping sliding windows with a stride of 20 and average the overlapping predictions to reconstruct the full-size map.

### 3. Method

The overall pipeline was: fundus image → preprocessing → DR grading → vessel segmentation and microaneurysm detection → visualization and assisted interpretation.

For DR binary classification, I used VisionFM, a multimodal multi-task ophthalmic foundation model. Its idea is to pretrain on large-scale ophthalmic images to learn general retinal representations, then transfer to downstream tasks. Compared with training from scratch, this transfer approach is more effective when labeled data is limited. I built the binary classification pipeline on top of it: I take the CLS tokens from the last four encoder layers, concatenate them into a feature vector, and feed it into a three-layer MLP head. The input size is 224×224 and the VisionFM fundus pretrained weights are loaded for fine-tuning.

For microaneurysm segmentation, I reproduced the training and testing pipeline of a published microaneurysm segmentation paper from its open-source code (the IDRiD segmentation branch). Its backbone is MSRNet, a multi-scale residual network with SE attention modules, trained as patch-level binary classification with CrossEntropyLoss, using Adam with a learning rate of 1e-3 for the first 150 epochs and 1e-4 afterwards. We chose a patch-based approach rather than segmenting the full image directly, because a microaneurysm often occupies only a few pixels in a full fundus image and simply disappears during downsampling. With 96×96 patches, the lesion occupies a much larger fraction of the input, so the model has a chance to learn it. At inference we reconstruct the full image from overlapping windows.

For vessel segmentation, I also used the VisionFM ViT encoder as the backbone, took the patch tokens from layers 3, 5, 7 and 11 as multi-scale features, and attached a UNETR decoder head. The input size was 512×512, and the output was upsampled back to the original resolution.

Everything was implemented in PyTorch. For evaluation we used Accuracy, F1, AUC and AP for classification, and Dice and IoU (Jaccard) for segmentation.

### 4. What I Actually Coded

As the principal investigator, I was responsible for the DR classification and lesion segmentation algorithms. Specifically:

- Data pipeline: I wrote the code for data reading, cleaning, augmentation and patient-level splitting, and built the PyTorch Dataset and DataLoader
- DR grading: I reproduced and adapted the VisionFM classification pipeline, including training, validation and test scripts
- Microaneurysm detection and segmentation: I reproduced the multi-scale segmentation network and ran the detection and segmentation experiments on small lesions
- Evaluation: I wrote the metric computation code, performed error sample analysis, and compared results under different settings
- Visualization: I overlaid the original image, prediction heatmaps and segmentation masks, to check whether the model was actually looking at the right region
- Platform: I took part in designing the model-invocation workflow of the prototype platform, and introduced a large language model to generate textual interpretations of the classification and segmentation results

On division of labour: the team had five members and I was the principal investigator. **All model training, evaluation and experimental analysis across the three task lines were done by me alone**, including data preprocessing, fine-tuning for binary classification and vessel segmentation, and the reproduction and hyperparameter tuning of the microaneurysm branch. One teammate built the front-end pages of the web prototype, and the remaining three wrote parts of the project report.

### 5. Results

Results on the internal held-out test sets are as follows.

**DR binary classification**: AP 93.35%, Accuracy 80.70%, AUC 85.73%, F1 83.58%.

One note on the AP: the test set is balanced 50:50, so the random baseline for the PR curve is 0.50, which means an AP of 93.35% is a clear improvement over chance. That said, the training script computes AP with macro averaging — `average_precision_score(target_one_hot, output, average='macro')` — i.e. AP is computed separately for each class and then averaged, rather than the standard binary average precision. On balanced data the two are close, but this should be stated if asked.

**Vessel segmentation**: Dice 78.17% on DRIVE.

**Microaneurysm detection and segmentation**: we reproduced the method and got the pipeline running, but **the evaluation protocol has problems, so we do not draw conclusions from its metrics and I would not quote numbers for this branch** — only describe the method. There are two reasons.

First, `dice_average_sets` in the original paper's code receives the full image flattened into a one-dimensional pixel array, so "average Dice over samples" becomes "average Dice over individual pixels". With the smoothing term this equals exactly `1 - 0.5×(1-accuracy)`, which is determined by accuracy alone — a model that outputs all background scores higher, so it is not a segmentation metric at all.

Second, solving the confusion matrix from Sensitivity 0.6083, Specificity 0.6850 and Accuracy 0.6371 implies that **62.45% of the evaluated pixels are positive**; the Precision, Dice and Jaccard derived from that same matrix match the reported values exactly, confirming they all come from one confusion matrix. But microaneurysms occupy well under 1% of pixels in a fundus image, so 62% is impossible — **the ground truth used at evaluation time was almost certainly the wrong mask file**. The exact cause can no longer be traced, since training ran on a server and the logs are gone.

The honest way to present this branch is therefore: the method pipeline works, but the evaluation is not yet rigorous, and this is the part that most needs redoing.

The metric structure itself tells us the dominant error mode. For microaneurysms, Sensitivity is 0.6083, clearly below Precision at 0.7625, which means **the errors are mainly missed detections rather than false alarms** — the model tends to predict conservatively, preferring to miss a lesion rather than report one. The threshold setting points the same way: we deliberately lowered the binarization threshold to 0.15 in exchange for sensitivity, and sensitivity still only reached 0.61, which means the bottleneck is not the threshold but the model's ability to discriminate targets this small.

The vessel segmentation branch shows a related but different issue: a Dice of 78.17% means overall coverage is reasonable, but thin vessel endings are prone to breaking or being missed, because very fine vessels lose detail through repeated downsampling.

For completeness: what we actually did was per-case visual inspection, overlaying the predicted masks on the original images and comparing them. We **did not perform a systematic stratified analysis**, for instance breaking down error rates by disease grade, image quality or lesion size. That is the part I would most want to add if I revisited the project.

### 6. Limitations

First, the data scale and sources are limited, and each task line was validated on only a single dataset: DR binary classification only on the organized version of Diabetic Retinopathy Arranged, vessel segmentation only on DRIVE, and microaneurysm only on IDRiD. None of them was tested across datasets. Medical imaging models are especially prone to degrading when the imaging device or population changes, which is the domain shift problem. This is the most important experiment we still need to run.

Second, the segmentation accuracy of very small lesions such as microaneurysms is highly sensitive to image resolution and annotation quality. Different annotators may disagree on where a lesion boundary lies, and this annotation noise directly affects model performance.

Third, interpretability is insufficient. We introduced a large language model to interpret the outputs, but this is essentially a second-order description of the results, **which is not the same as making the model itself interpretable**. If the model predicts incorrectly, the language model may produce a plausible-sounding but wrong explanation, which carries a hallucination risk. What is really needed is for the model's attention or gradients to localize on the true lesion regions, and we have not fully achieved that.

Fourth, the system remains a prototype and has not been evaluated in a real clinical setting. The prototype validates that the pipeline runs, not that it is clinically effective. Demonstrating real value would require collaboration with hospitals and prospective validation in actual screening scenarios.

Fifth, the evaluation protocol itself needs fixing, and this was the most serious problem of the three branches. For microaneurysms we inherited the evaluation script from the paper's code, and only discovered on review that two things were wrong: its Dice degenerates into a per-pixel average during full-image inference, so the value is determined by accuracy alone and is not a segmentation metric at all; and solving the confusion matrix implies that 62% of the evaluated pixels are positive, which contradicts how sparse microaneurysms are, meaning the ground truth was almost certainly loaded from the wrong file. We therefore do not treat that branch's metrics as a conclusion. The lesson is: **get the evaluation metric right before talking about model performance** — with the wrong metric, even a good model is only talking to itself.

Sixth, the train/test split was not strict enough. The DR binary task was split randomly by image rather than by patient. The file names carry patient IDs (for example `10047_left` and `10047_right` are the two eyes of the same patient), and we found that 286 patients have one eye in the training set and the other in the test set — about 26% of all patients in the test set. The two eyes of a patient are highly correlated in disease severity and imaging conditions, so this makes the test metrics optimistic. The correct approach is a patient-grouped split, ensuring each patient appears in only one set. We only became aware of this during our post-hoc review.

One closing note: this project ran under limited time and compute, so all three pipelines only reached the stage of running end-to-end with initial validation, and many experiments were left unfinished. If I continued the work, I would prioritise a patient-grouped split and cross-dataset validation — neither change is complex, but together they would make the conclusions far more reliable.

| 那你会怎么改进？ | 按患者 ID 分组划分，保证同一患者只出现在一个集合里；再补一套来自不同数据集的独立外部验证 |
| 为什么选 VisionFM 而不是从零训练？ | 医学标注数据稀缺，迁移学习能降低对标注量的依赖；预训练学到的眼底表征有跨任务泛化能力 |
| 微动脉瘤为什么难分割？ | 目标极小（几个像素）、与背景对比度低、不同病灶形态差异大，且下采样会直接丢失目标 |
| 数据不平衡怎么处理？ | 数据本身就是 50:50 均衡的（训练 2264/2265，测试 568/567），不需要额外处理——这是个可以干脆回答的问题 |
| 数据集不算大，会不会过拟合？ | ① 我们是基于 VisionFM 的预训练权重做微调，编码器已经学到了眼底表征，不是从零训练，对数据量的依赖低得多；② 判断过拟合要看训练集和验证集曲线是否分离，不能只看测试指标；③ 小数据集上微调基础模型，过拟合确实是主要风险，VisionFM 官方文档本身就提示 IDRiD、MESSIDOR2 这类数据集只微调 5 个 epoch，再长就会过拟合；④ 我们的做法是用验证集选最优 checkpoint，再在留出的测试集上评估 |
| 为什么 AP 93.35% 比 AUC 85.73% 还高？ | 测试集是均衡的，PR 的随机基线是 0.50，所以 AP 相对基线提升明显；但脚本里 AP 用的是 macro 平均（对两类分别求 AP 再平均），不是标准二分类 AP，被问到时要说清口径 |
| 图像是怎么预处理的？ | 原图 1024×768，统一缩放到 224×224（分割 512×512），**没有保持长宽比**，之后按 Fundus 模态的均值方差标准化 |
| 学习率、batch size 怎么定的？ | 要能说出具体设置和调整依据，不能答"随便试的" |
| Dice 和 IoU 的区别？ | Dice = 2\|A∩B\|/(\|A\|+\|B\|)，IoU = \|A∩B\|/\|A∪B\|，两者单调相关，Dice 对重叠更敏感、数值普遍偏高 |
| 微动脉瘤的分割指标是多少？ | "这一支我们完成了方法复现和流程跑通，但评估口径还不够严谨——当时沿用的评测脚本对极小目标不太适用，所以指标我不倾向于下结论。"**不要背具体数字** |
| 如果被追问 0.8185 或 0.6767 | 0.8185 是逐像素平均的产物，恒等于 `1-0.5×(1-准确率)`，只由准确率决定，全输出背景反而更高；0.6767 虽与 F1、Jaccard 自洽，但反推出评测集正样本占 62%，与微动脉瘤的稀疏性矛盾，说明 ground truth 加载错了。能讲清这两条是加分项 |
| 大模型辅助解读会不会产生幻觉？ | 会，这正是我们认识的不足之一，见第六段 |
| 用大模型解读结果，算不算你的创新点？ | 要诚实：这是工程层面的增强，提升了结果可读性，但**不是算法层面的创新**，不要过度包装 |
| 项目里最有难度的地方是什么？ | 建议答小目标分割，并给出具体的失败尝试和最后的解决思路 |
| 如果重做一次，你会怎么改？ | 按顺序说：① 数据划分改成按患者分组；② 补跨数据集验证；③ 微动脉瘤改用病灶级 FROC 评估并尝试更强的多尺度方法；④ 补充与医生的对照评估 |

## 已从代码仓库核实的事实

以下信息来自代码仓库（OphthaVisionLab、Microaneurysm-Detection）的直接确认，可放心使用：

- DR 二分类：ViT 编码器 + 取最后 4 层 CLS token 拼接 + 3 层 MLP 分类头；输入 224×224；checkpoint 目录名 `single_cls_260106_Binary_finetune`
- 血管分割：VisionFM ViT 编码器 + UNETR 解码头；取第 3、5、7、11 层 patch token；输入 512×512；checkpoint `checkpoint_108_linear.pth`；DRIVE 数据集 Dice 0.7817
- 微动脉瘤：MSRNet（多尺度残差网络 + SE 模块）；patch 96×96；训练 patch 数 190000；batch 30；Adam，学习率 1e-3→1e-4（第 150 轮切换）；CrossEntropyLoss；测试时 stride 20 重叠重建；二值化阈值 0.15；IDRiD 微动脉瘤子集，测试 27 张图
- 微动脉瘤评估（**有问题，不要引用这些数字**）：`model_test.py` 中 Dice 由 `dice_average_sets` 计算，接收的是一维像素数组，因此退化成逐像素平均；反推混淆矩阵得出评测集正样本占 62.45%，与微动脉瘤的稀疏性矛盾，说明当时 ground truth 加载有误
- DR 二分类数据：Diabetic Retinopathy Arranged，标签 `路径;0/1`。训练 4529 张（正 2264 / 负 2265），测试 1135 张（正 568 / 负 567），两边都是 50:50；原图 1024×768；训练与测试文件名零重叠，但 **286 名患者的左右眼跨集合**

## 细节追问的标准答法

老师手上只有简历，具体数字和过程都由你来讲。原则是：**确定的部分讲具体，不确定的部分坦率带过，不要现编。**

### 被问"这个数据集你处理了什么"

> "用的是公开的 Diabetic Retinopathy Arranged 数据集。我下载后整理成了自己的目录结构：标签是二分类的 0 和 1，分别对应健康和 DR 阳性；按大约 8:2 划出训练集和测试集，训练集 4529 张、测试集 1135 张，两个集合的正负比例都接近 1:1；然后生成训练脚本直接读取的标签文件。图像原始尺寸是 1024×768，训练时由数据加载流程统一缩放到 224×224，再按 Fundus 模态的均值和方差做标准化。"

如果被追问"有没有做去重、剔除低质量图"：

> "这部分细节我记不太清了，因为距离做这个项目已经有一年。我能确定的是数据本身是均衡的，训练集和测试集之间没有同名文件。"

**不要**为了把回答填满而编造 CLAHE、直方图均衡之类的步骤——每编一个，后面就会多三个问题。

### 被问"为什么数据是 1:1 均衡的"

> "这个数据集上游就做过均衡整理，所以训练集和测试集的正负比例都接近 1:1。真实筛查场景里阳性率远低于这个水平，所以我们的指标会偏乐观一些。"

（如果确实是你自己下采样得到的，就如实说是下采样。两种说法都不影响后面的内容。）

### 被问"测试集算独立验证吗"

> "是从同一数据源按 8:2 划出来的留出集，和训练集同源，严格说不是外部验证集。这也是我们当时的一个局限。"

### 被问"如果给你更多时间，你会怎么改"

> "当时受时间和算力限制，我只把三条链路跑通并做了初步验证。如果继续做，我会按这个顺序补：第一，把数据划分改成按患者分组，保证同一患者只出现在一个集合里；第二，补跨数据集的验证，检验模型的泛化能力；第三，微动脉瘤那一支改用按病灶级别的 FROC 评估，并尝试更强的多尺度方法。"

### 被问"IDRiD 那一版的结果"（按这个说，不要报 0.7915）

IDRiD 那一版是在服务器上跑的，日志已经没有了，所以不要报它的具体数字。把它讲成"为什么换数据集"的过程：

> "我们最开始是在 IDRiD 上做 DR 分级的。IDRiD 只有 516 张图，五级分布很不均衡——Healthy 和 DR-2 各有 168 张，但 DR-1 只有 25 张，这个类别基本没法训练。并成二分类之后正样本又占到 67%，在这种分布下 F1 对阈值非常敏感，指标不稳定，所以后来的正式实验我们换用了 Diabetic Retinopathy Arranged 数据集：规模更大，而且正负样本接近 1:1，指标才能真正反映模型能力。"

这段话不需要日志支撑，它讲的都是可以从数据集本身推出来的事实，而且展示了你理解**数据分布如何影响指标**——这本身就是加分项。

### 被问到确实记不清的细节（通用模板）

> "训练是在服务器上做的，日志没有保留下来，所以这个具体参数我记不太清了。我能确定的是……"

**关键是把问题从"细节记忆"转到"设计判断"。** 老师问"你当时用了什么学习率"，真正想知道的不是那个数字，而是你懂不懂学习率在训练里起什么作用。所以你可以这样接：

> "……我能确定的是我们用的是基于 VisionFM 预训练权重的微调流程，这一类小数据集微调通常用比较小的学习率、只跑很少的轮数，因为 VisionFM 官方文档里明确提到 IDRiD、MESSIDOR2 这些数据集只微调 5 个 epoch 就够，再长就会过拟合。"

记忆有缺口不扣分，**现编数字被追问时崩掉才扣分**。面试官自己也读过研，一年前的项目记不清细节是常态。

| 任务 | 要报的数字 |

### 表达策略

**先说结构。** 开场就把三条线的数据集分清（Diabetic Retinopathy Arranged / DRIVE / IDRiD），别让老师以为是一个数据集跑通了三个任务。

**分工主动讲。** 团队 5 人，模型的训练、评估和实验分析全部由你完成，1 人做前端、3 人写报告。这个分工对你很有利，所有技术细节都落在你身上。

**两个短板不主动提，但要准备好答法。** 一是患者级划分（286 名患者的左右眼跨了训练集和测试集），二是均衡测试集的临床代表性。这两个都属于"做得不够的地方"，简历上看不出来。如果被问到，**照实承认并立刻接上正确做法**，比如"划分是按图片随机做的，正确做法应该按患者分组，这是当时考虑不周的地方"。承认加给出正确方法，比含糊或否认都安全——眼科方向的老师看到文件名里的 `_left`/`_right` 加数字编号，很容易想到患者 ID 这一层。

**收尾用未来计划。** 面试官往往会给一个"如果重来你会怎么做"的机会。这时候把上面两条短板主动转成计划说出来，效果最好——既显得你想清楚了，又不需要在任何具体细节上硬撑。
