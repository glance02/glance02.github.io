# 英文自我介绍（雷显秋）

> 用途：推免 / 夏令营 / 预推免面试开场
> 语速参考：1 分钟版约 150 词，2 分钟版约 300 词，按每分钟 140-150 词的语速朗读
> 学校名称处写成 `[university]`，面试前替换成目标学校

## 一、1 分钟版（首选，绝大多数场合够用）

Good morning, professors. It's a great honor to be here.

My name is Lei Xianqiu. I'm a third-year undergraduate majoring in Computer Science and Technology at China University of Mining and Technology.

My GPA is 4.42 out of 5, ranking 21st among 191 students, with full marks in Advanced Mathematics and Data Structure Labs.

My main research experience is a student innovation project on intelligent diagnosis of chronic fundus diseases, where I'm the principal investigator. I reproduced and adapted deep learning models for diabetic retinopathy grading and microaneurysm segmentation, building on the VisionFM ophthalmic foundation model with PyTorch.

I also received an Honorable Mention in the Mathematical Contest in Modeling and a provincial second prize in the Lanqiao Cup.

I'm strongly interested in deep learning, medical image analysis and trustworthy AI, and I hope to pursue my master's degree at `[university]`.

Thank you.

### 中文对照

各位老师上午好，很荣幸能够参加这次面试。

我叫雷显秋，是中国矿业大学计算机科学与技术专业的本科生。

我的 GPA 是 4.42/5，专业排名 21/191，高等数学和数据结构实验都是满分。

我的主要科研经历是一个关于慢性眼底病变智能诊断的大学生创新项目，我担任第一负责人。我复现并适配了用于糖尿病视网膜病变分级和微动脉瘤分割的深度学习模型，基于 VisionFM 眼科基础模型，用 PyTorch 完成训练与评估。

我还获得过美国大学生数学建模竞赛 H 奖和蓝桥杯省赛二等奖。

我对深度学习、医学图像分析和可信人工智能有浓厚兴趣，希望能在 `[学校]` 攻读硕士学位。

谢谢。

## 二、2 分钟版（老师明确说"详细介绍一下"时用）

Good morning, professors. It's a great honor to have this interview with you.

My name is Lei Xianqiu, a third-year undergraduate majoring in Computer Science and Technology at China University of Mining and Technology.

Academically, my GPA is 4.42 out of 5, ranking 21st among 191 students, with a weighted average score of 94.32 out of 100. Advanced Mathematics and Data Structure Labs were both full marks, which gave me a solid mathematical and systematic foundation for research.

My main research experience is a student innovation project named "Intelligent Diagnosis System for Chronic Fundus Diseases Based on Deep Learning", where I serve as the principal investigator. Diabetic retinopathy is a leading cause of blindness, and screening is limited by the shortage of ophthalmologists. My work focuses on diabetic retinopathy grading and lesion segmentation. I reproduced and adapted deep learning models based on the VisionFM ophthalmic foundation model, and implemented microaneurysm detection with a multi-scale segmentation-to-classification network, using PyTorch for training, evaluation and visualization. I also helped design the model-invocation workflow of a prototype clinical platform, where we introduced a large language model to interpret the diagnostic results. This taught me that research should not stop at benchmark numbers, but should also consider interpretability and clinical usability.

I also trained my problem-abstracting ability through mathematical modeling. In the Mathematical Contest in Modeling, we studied a mixed elimination mechanism combining judge scores and audience votes. Since the real votes were unobservable, we modeled audience support as a latent variable and used Bayesian inversion with historical records. I was responsible for modeling, implementation and paper writing, using MCMC with Plackett-Luce and Bradley-Terry models to estimate audience preferences. Our paper received an Honorable Mention.

My research interests are deep learning, computer vision, medical image analysis and trustworthy AI. I hope to pursue my master's degree at `[university]`, where I can strengthen my theoretical foundation and take part in research that is effective, reliable and interpretable.

Thank you for your time.

## 三、替换末尾句（按学校调整）

- **通用**：I hope to pursue my master's degree at `[university]` and grow into a researcher who can turn algorithms into real solutions.
- **湖南大学**：Hunan University has a strong group in AI for Science and medical AI, which matches my interest in medical image analysis. I would be glad to continue my research here.
- **强调读博意愿**：If I find that research gives me continuous growth, I'm also willing to pursue a PhD.

## 四、发音和背诵提示

容易读错的词：diabetic retinopathy（视网膜病变）、microaneurysm（微动脉瘤）、ophthalmic（眼科的）、latent variable（隐变量）、trustworthy（可信的）、Honorable Mention（荣誉提名）。

停顿位置：每说完一个要点停一拍，尤其是 GPA 那句和项目那句之间，让老师有时间跟上。

不要在自我介绍里塞满信息。老师手上已经有你的简历，自我介绍的作用是给出一个清晰的框架，引导他们往你想被问的地方提问。

## 五、可能被追问的点（提前准备）

自我介绍里每句话都可能成为提问入口，以下是最危险的四句：

1. "principal investigator" — 追问你在团队里具体做了什么、怎么分工、其他人做了什么
2. "VisionFM ophthalmic foundation model" — 追问这个模型的结构、预训练方式、你为什么选它
3. "microaneurysm segmentation" — 追问为什么这个病灶难分割、你用了什么方法、效果如何
4. "trustworthy AI" — 追问你理解的可信 AI 包含哪些维度，别只说"可解释性"一个词

另外，2021 年有考生被明确追问"你负责算法设计还是写代码、这个项目你写代码了吗"，所以 2 分钟版里"using PyTorch for training, evaluation and visualization"这句背后要有具体的代码细节支撑，不能停留在概念层面。
